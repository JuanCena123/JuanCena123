Current Focus

The project that I am currently focused on is a structure from motion program or notebook. The concept is not exactly state of the art as far as I am aware, however I find it very interesting. I originally started working on this about a year ago for a creative inquiry that I was apart of at Clemson that introduced students to machine learning and gave them the tools to work on their own projects. I did not give the class the attention that I should've and my projects never got to where they should've been, hence my working on them now. In hope of doing things right this time I am reading Packt's opencv4 textbook to gain a deeper understanding of the underlying concepts that make sfm possible as I begin to code the notebook. The data/competition is from Kaggle, more specifically googles/happiers yearly image matching competition. [1/10/2025] Originally I was doing the 2023 challenge, but it has dawned upon me that I have a lot to learn about the subject and the research field has a lot more depth than I originally thought. So, I will be starting with the earliest competition they have on kaggle which to my knowledge is much simpler then the 2023 sfm competition. More to come, this is becoming a bit of a diary lol.
